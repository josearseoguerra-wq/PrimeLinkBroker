QUÉ HACEMOS

This platform serves as the official informational portal for United Trade Solutions LLC and Prime Commercial Link LLC. While each entity operates independently under its own registered business purpose, both share a core expertise in trade facilitation, wholesale procurement and commercial settlement — purchasing from primary suppliers on behalf of retail buyers and partner agencies, and coordinating delivery directly to the end recipient.

Esta plataforma funciona como el portal informativo oficial de United Trade Solutions LLC y Prime Commercial Link LLC. Aunque cada entidad opera de forma independiente bajo su propio giro comercial registrado, ambas comparten especialización en facilitación comercial, compras al por mayor y liquidación de transacciones — comprando a proveedores principales en nombre de compradores minoristas y agencias asociadas, y coordinando la entrega directamente al destinatario final.

PAYMENT INFRASTRUCTURE

INFRAESTRUCTURA DE PAGOS

Settlement through institutional banking channels.

Liquidación mediante canales bancarios institucionales.

To ensure maximum security and transparency, every transaction is processed through authorized banking channels — never through this website.

Para garantizar máxima seguridad y transparencia, cada transacción se procesa a través de canales bancarios autorizados — nunca a través de este sitio web.

Inbound Payment Channels

Canales de Recepción de Fondos

Payments received from clients & agencies

Pagos recibidos de clientes y agencias

  • Authorized commercial Zelle (retail collection only)
  • Zelle comercial autorizado (solo recepción minorista)
  • Direct bank transfers (Wire / ACH)
  • Transferencias bancarias directas (Wire / ACH)
  • Direct bank deposits
  • Depósitos bancarios directos
  • Money orders and bank drafts
  • Giros bancarios y postales

Outbound Wholesale Disbursements

Desembolsos Mayoristas

Payments issued to primary suppliers

Pagos emitidos a proveedores principales

  • Direct bank wire transfers (Wire / ACH)
  • Transferencias bancarias directas (Wire / ACH)
  • Direct corporate bank deposits
  • Depósitos bancarios corporativos
  • Official bank drafts / money orders
  • Giros bancarios / cheques oficiales

Note: wholesale disbursements to suppliers are never executed via Zelle.

Nota: los desembolsos mayoristas a proveedores nunca se ejecutan mediante Zelle.
